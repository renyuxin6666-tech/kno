Both video tracks are 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 and 30.500 seconds, with AAC 48 kHz stereo audio. The production loudness target is −16.5 LUFS; the finished main version measures approximately −16.6 LUFS. These assets accompany v0.2.1; the original v0.2.0 end card is preserved. Demonstration screens are labeled, and personal material is redacted.

Videos are supplied as Release assets. The source code's MIT license does not establish rights to third-party synthesized voices or other media; the production record leaves public and commercial use of the narration service unconfirmed. Installation and scheduled upkeep require the corresponding local software, folder access and a working Codex desktop environment.
